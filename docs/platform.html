Step 1 · Book

Build the load from the paperwork, not the keyboard.

Drop in the customer's rate confirmation, tender or email. ROIN reads it and fills in the load: customer, stops, dates, references, freight and rate. You check it and save.

Anything it isn't sure of is highlighted, and every value shows the line it came from, so checking takes a glance instead of a re-type.

Step 2 · Cover

Find the right truck and lock it in.

ROIN puts the carriers who already run the lane next to the trucks that are open near the pickup, and tells you why each one made the list.

Carriers ranked for the load

Ranked by how often they've run the lane for you, whether they have a truck nearby, and how soon it's free.

Open trucks, posted

Post the trucks you know about, or email a carrier a link to post their own. ROIN matches them to loads within 150 miles.

Insurance at a glance

Every carrier shows whether their insurance is active, expiring in the next 45 days or expired, and whether their packet is in.

Rate con signed online

Send the rate confirmation from the load. The carrier signs it online, with a copy to your dispatcher if you like.

Step 3 · Track

Know where every load is without calling around.

Check calls, documents and every change land on the load as they happen, with who did what and when. Anyone on the team can open it and see where things stand.

Check calls by text

ROIN texts the driver for an update on a schedule you set, or when you tap "Ping driver now." The reply lands on the load.

A tracking link for your customer

Send one link that shows status, stops, last location, ETA and the POD once it's delivered. No prices. You can see when they open it and turn it off any time.

PODs without the chase

Text or email the driver a link to upload the POD straight to the load, one for each delivery stop.

Every document in one place

Tender, rate con, BOL, carrier invoice and POD sit on the load. Drop a file in and ROIN works out what it is and files it.

Step 4 · Get paid

Close the load and the books close with it.

The customer invoice, the carrier's pay and your margin are all part of the load. When you settle it, your reports update on the spot. There is nothing to add up at the end of the month.
