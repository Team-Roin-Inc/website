Step 2 · Cover

Find the right truck and lock it in.

Search carriers

Look up a carrier from inside the load and put them on it in a click. ROIN ranks the carriers who have run that lane for you before.

Authority checked first

ROIN checks a carrier's operating authority before they can be put on a load, so a carrier without it never slips through.

Send it out

Send the load out to the carrier, then log each status as it moves. What you tell the customer and what the team sees always match.

Step 3 · Track

Know where every load is without calling around.

Tracking updates, check calls and paperwork land on the load as they happen, with who did what and when. Anyone on the team can open it and see where things stand.

BookedCustomer, lane, windows and references entered once.
CoveredCarrier assigned, authority checked, rate confirmed.
In transitGPS positions and check calls logged on the load.
DeliveredPOD and documents attached to the same record.
SettledInvoice, carrier pay and margin closed out.
Step 4 · Get paid

Close the load and the books close with it.

The customer invoice, the carrier's pay and your margin are all part of the load. When you settle it, your reports update on the spot. There is nothing to add up at the end of the month.

It all flows to QuickBooks Online, so your accountant keeps working where they already do.
