02 · The load

One page per load, start to finish.

Open a load and everything about it is there, one tab at a time. Every check call, status change, document and payment also lands on one timeline, with who did it and when. The parts marked below are the jobs ROIN does for you.

Load 53318Load infoCarrierDocumentsTrackingAR / AP
Load info
ROIN does this

Drop in the customer's tender or rate con and ROIN fills in the load: stops, dates, references, freight and rate. It suggests what to charge and what to pay from what that lane has paid you before. You check it and save.

Carrier
ROIN does this

ROIN lists the carriers who have run the lane for you and the open trucks near the pickup, and says why each one made the list. New carriers sign your packet and send insurance from one link, and the rate con goes out for signature from the load.

Documents

A checklist of every document the load needs, each marked on file, awaiting signature or still missing. Drivers upload the POD from a link you text or email them.

Tracking

Where the truck is now, the ETA, miles to go and the route on a map. Location comes in on its own from MacroPoint, from a text to the driver, or from a check call you log in a few clicks. Send your customer a tracking link that shows all of it, without the prices.

AR / AP
ROIN does this

Email the invoice with the POD attached in one step. ROIN reads the carrier's invoice so you can check it against what you agreed before you pay. Settle the load and your reports, commissions and QuickBooks are up to date.

03 · The business

Know what a lane pays before you quote it.

Every load you run becomes lane history: what you charged, what you paid and your margin, month by month for two years. Ask a question in plain English, like "What was our profit in August?", and get the answer from your own loads.

The dashboard shows revenue, profit, margin and on-time performance against last month. Reports can be filtered, saved, shared and sent to Excel.
