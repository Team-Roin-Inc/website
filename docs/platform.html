The platform

A TMS that takes on more of the work every release.

ROIN runs the load from booking to settlement, and puts the system to work on the jobs brokers still do by hand: reading paperwork, pricing lanes, finding trucks and choosing carriers.

The system does the busywork

01

Rate intelligence

Every rate you have quoted and paid becomes history you can use. See what a lane has cost over time and what a load should be getting before you quote it.

— Lane rate history, charted — Suggested buy and sell rate per load — Margin tracked against the market you actually run
02

Document intake

Upload a rate confirmation, BOL or carrier invoice. ROIN reads it and fills it in against the right load, so the paperwork stops being a data-entry job.

— Reads rate cons, BOLs and invoices — Matches the document to the load — Flags what it could not read for a person to check
03

Capacity posting

Post the trucks you have, by area or against a specific load, so coverage starts before the phone calls do.

— Post capacity by region — Post against a single load — Visible from the board
04

Carrier matching

Rank the carriers most likely to take a load well, based on the lanes and loads they have already run for you.

— Ranked by your own history — Authority checked before assignment — One click from the load

Every load, visible start to finish

One record follows the load from quote to settlement. Tracking, status changes, documents and money all land on it as they happen, with who did what and when. Anyone on the team can open it and see exactly where things stand.

BookedCustomer, lane, windows and references entered once.
CoveredCarrier assigned, authority checked, rate confirmed.
In transitGPS positions and check calls logged on the load.
DeliveredPOD and documents attached to the same record.
SettledInvoice, carrier pay and margin closed out.

Cover the load without a second system

Search carriers

Look up a carrier from inside the load and assign them without copying numbers between screens.

Authority checked first

Operating authority is verified before a carrier can be assigned, so an unauthorized carrier cannot quietly end up on your load.

Tender and update

Send the load out, then log each status as it moves. The customer-facing answer and the internal record are the same thing.

The money side closes itself out

Rate breakdown, customer invoice and carrier payable are part of the load, not a separate ledger you reconcile later. Because the reports read the same rows, settling a load is what updates them — there is no month-end export step.
